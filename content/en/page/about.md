Hey, I'm Andy Clifton. 43 years old, Cloud Engineer and Tribe Lead at [RawWorks](https://www.rawworks.nl/), Microsoft Certified Trainer, and the voice behind the Azure Adventurer podcast. This site is where I write down what I run into in practice, so you don't have to figure it out on your own.

## What I do

At RawWorks I help organisations set up their Microsoft cloud environment properly, and more importantly keep it that way. That usually starts with the foundation: an Azure landing zone built on the Cloud Adoption Framework, with governance, networking and identity in place before a single workload runs. Then comes the rest: migrations, hybrid environments with Azure Arc and Azure Local, Identity and Access Management with Entra ID, and the security layer on top with Defender and Conditional Access.

My clients are mostly in government, healthcare and enterprise, including several Dutch water authorities and hospitals. Those are environments where you don't just try things out: every outbound connection has to be justified, every change goes through a change process, and "works on my machine" doesn't count as evidence. That's exactly where it gets interesting. Almost every blog on this site comes out of a real project, including the pitfalls I hit along the way.

As Tribe Lead I'm also responsible for the technical growth of our team. I organise knowledge sessions, deep dives and hands on sessions, both within RawWorks and together with our sister companies within Interstellar.

## Training and speaking

As a Microsoft Certified Trainer I regularly deliver training, from Azure Fundamentals for people just getting started to deep dives on Defender, hybrid cloud and FinOps. I also speak at events and customer sessions on a regular basis. When I explain something, I want you to be able to apply it afterwards, not just to have heard it.

## Azure Adventurer

On the [Azure Adventurer](https://www.youtube.com/@AzureAdventurer) podcast I talk about what's moving in the Azure ecosystem: new services, public previews, and what they actually mean for administrators and architects. No marketing talk, just the questions you ask yourself when you have to work with it.

## Why this blog

I write because the blogs that helped me most were the ones that were honest about what did and didn't work. So that's what I try to be here. You'll find step by step guides, deep dives on new Azure functionality, and the occasional opinion on where Microsoft is heading. Most of my writing is in Dutch, because there's plenty of English content already and not nearly enough in my own language; the English section is where I translate the posts that deserve a wider audience.

## Outside work

I live in Tilburg, in the Netherlands. At home I tinker with home automation and energy management around the solar panels, and on weekends you'll find me on the sideline coaching a youth football team. That last one may well be the best training in patience and explaining things you can get.
