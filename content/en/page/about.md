Hey, I'm Andy Clifton. Tech Lead at [RawWorks](https://www.rawworks.nl/), Microsoft Certified Trainer and the voice behind the Azure Adventurer podcast. I help organizations govern their datacenter and Azure as one platform, with Azure Arc and Azure Local on a solid CAF foundation. This site is where I write down what I run into in practice, so you don't have to figure it out yourself.

## What I write and speak about

**Hybrid Azure.** Azure Arc and Azure Local in practice: from onboarding and the Arc gateway to security and operations at scale.

**Governance.** Landing zones based on the Cloud Adoption Framework, policy as code and assessing existing environments.

**Sovereignty.** How to stay in control of data and workloads without giving up the benefits of Azure.

## What I do

At RawWorks I help organizations set up their Microsoft cloud environment properly and, above all, keep it that way. That starts with the foundation: an Azure landing zone based on the Cloud Adoption Framework, with governance, networking and identity in order before a single workload runs. From that base I extend the same rules to everything outside Azure, using Azure Arc and Azure Local.

My customers are mainly in government, healthcare and the private sector, including several Dutch water authorities and hospitals. These are environments where you don't just try things out: every outbound connection has to be justified, every change goes through a change process, and "it works on my machine" doesn't count as proof. That is exactly where it gets interesting. Almost all the posts on this site come from real projects, including the pitfalls I ran into along the way.

As Tech Lead I am also responsible for the technical development of our team. I organize knowledge sessions, deep dives and hands on sessions, both within RawWorks and together with our sister companies within Interstellar.

## Speaking and training

I enjoy speaking at meetups and conferences about hybrid Azure and governance. Most recently I spoke at Fusion Forward 2026. Looking for a speaker? Send me a message on LinkedIn.

As a Microsoft Certified Trainer I regularly deliver trainings, from Azure Fundamentals to in depth sessions on Defender, hybrid cloud and FinOps. When I explain something, I want you to be able to apply it yourself afterwards, not just to have heard it.

## Azure Adventurer

In the [Azure Adventurer](https://www.youtube.com/@AzureAdventurer) podcast I talk about what is moving in the Azure ecosystem: new services, public previews, and what they mean in practice for administrators and architects. No marketing talk, just the questions you ask yourself when you actually have to work with it.

## Why this blog

I write because I have learned the most from other people's blogs that were honest about what did and did not work. So I try to do the same here. You will find step by step guides, deep dives into new Azure features and the occasional opinion on where Microsoft is heading. Everything is published in Dutch, because there is too little good content in our own language, and in English as well.
